# Example Brand

Replace this directory with one per brand you manage. Slug should match how the brand is referenced in MAP (lowercased, hyphenated).

## Identifiers

- **MAP brand_id:** _set after running `list_brands`_
- **Seller Central account(s):** _e.g., US, CA_
- **Amazon Ads integration_id:** _UUID from `list_amazon_ads_integrations`_
- **Selling Partner integration_id:** _UUID from `list_selling_partner_integrations`_
- **Top ASINs to watch:** _list parent + variants_

## Goals (current quarter)

- _e.g., grow Sponsored Products ROAS to 4.0_
- _e.g., reduce out-of-stock days to <5/mo_
- _e.g., launch 3 new ASINs_

## Notes

Anything Claude should know before acting on this brand: brand voice, pricing floors, "do not touch" campaigns, seasonal patterns, etc.
